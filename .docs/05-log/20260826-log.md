### The compliance finding

`rule.md` was written for "upload → we host → we delete", funded by ads. The product has no
server, no upload, no account, and no ads. Rather than deleting the mismatched rules, §4.1
records **which duties dissolve and why** — CCA §26 logging, random file ids and TTL jobs,
consent banners and ad-script gating, ETA §9/26 consent records.

Framed as **"architecture as compliance — the requirements this design removes"**, the same
document goes from being the weakest artefact in the submission to the strongest. Note in
particular that `rule.md` PDPA rule 23 *forbade* claiming "files never touch a server" — **and
it forbade it precisely because we accepted uploads and ran ads.** With neither, the claim is
true and verifiable. The premise is gone; the reasoning is recorded rather than dropped.
