2 · Set up once

  1. Extract the entire ZIP. Open this file locally. Keep the working copy outside Git and outside public/shared folders. Share only the untouched blank ZIP with teammates.
  2. One facilitator reads the cards; a second observer records on a separate laptop. The participant controls their own device. Two pairs can work concurrently while the fifth member coordinates.
  3. Run a team practice using the fictional worked example. Keep practice separate from U01–U15.
  4. Provide the six supplied synthetic files from fixtures/. Do not replace them with personal documents. The slides contain padding, so their compression ratio is not representative of ordinary slides.
  5. Open UniLab and record the revision from release.json. Keep the build fixed during a wave. Check the participant's actual device/browser and baseline tools before timing.
  6. Open sessions/U01/recorder.html for the first real participant, or their assigned folder. It has only the code prefilled. Read the complete notice and questionnaire protocol. The recorder stays local and requires an explicit CSV download to save.

3 · Welcome and obtain actual agreement · about 5 minutes

Thank you. We are testing how understandable and useful UniLab is, not your ability. Please use only these fictional files. We will record a participant code, task times and outcomes, observations and your questionnaire answers. Your document contents are processed on your device; we do not need copies. Raw study records stay in restricted team storage until 18 December 2026, then are deleted; reviewed aggregate results may appear in our coursework. You can skip questions or stop. Keep your code and contact the team member who invited you if you wish to withdraw before aggregation; after results are combined anonymously we may be unable to remove an individual contribution. Photos, audio or video are optional and require separate permission. Do you agree to participate and have coded observations recorded?

Explain in a language they understand. Answer questions before starting. Record the actual response, time, notice version, method and observer codes in consent-record.txt. Declining means no testing/data collection. Do not tick agreement on their behalf without their actual agreement. Photo/video permission is separate; no recording is required to participate. Do not record names, contacts, IDs or real signatures in this pack.

Give the participant their code and the recruiting member's existing contact channel. Confirm eligibility/duplicate status; let the coordinator resolve any uncertainty. Record prior experience as context.

4 · Four task cards · allow 25–35 minutes or longer

Read one card at a time. For each task, first observe their usual method, then UniLab with the same input and target. Start timing when they begin; stop after the saved result is opened and inspected. Note baseline tool and conditions. Let them stop if stuck; record elapsed time and the reason. Do not turn an unsuccessful baseline into a successful comparison time.

Read only the goal, not menu/button instructions. If asked for help, say: “What would you normally try?” If you provide guidance, record Assisted. Keep failures and assistance in the results. Baseline-first practice effects are a limitation to report.

5 · Questionnaires and debrief · about 7 minutes

Use the recorder's unchanged 10 SUS and 11 TAM items. The participant chooses their answers: 1 strongly disagree through 5 strongly agree. Explain the scale neutrally; do not suggest an answer. Skip means blank. If translating, pilot/review the wording and record the language/version.

What was hardest? What worked well? Would you use this again, and for what? What would you change first?

Capture only necessary, non-identifying observations. Check the session-complete box only after all four tasks were attempted/timed and required observations/consent/eligibility were verified. A completed session may include failures; questionnaire completeness is checked separately.

6 · Save before the participant leaves · about 3 minutes

  1. Export the recorder to session-U01.csv, using the actual participant code. Confirm the downloaded file exists and contains the expected code/date. Move it into that person's private session folder; avoid silently overwriting earlier evidence.
  2. Keep consent-record.txt and any permissioned evidence beside it. Write a coded reference in the recorder. Never send raw transcripts, identities or personal documents to an AI tool or a public repository.
  3. Record defects in defect-log.csv with severity, owner, evidence and later fix/retest revision. Ask the participant to verify their intended questionnaire choices if anything is unclear.
  4. Update recruitment-tracker.csv using actual status only. Retests go in private-records/retests and keep the original participant code.

7 · Repeat, review, calculate

Review after the first five people. Record any fix and new revision, then run the next five and the additional five. Report changed builds separately. Fifteen people × four attempts = 60 attempts, not 60 people. Report recruited, eligible, completed, attempted, successful and questionnaire counts separately.
