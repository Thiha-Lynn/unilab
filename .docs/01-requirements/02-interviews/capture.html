Before anything is written down

Who — role only

The six — one at a time, in this order

Q1

Think of the last file you had to hand in — what was it, and for which class?

Q2

Walk me through what you actually did — from the file existing to it being submitted.

Most pains live here — do not rush it. Silence is fine.
If they open a door
  • a website or app → "How did you pick that one? Has it ever stopped you partway?"
  • a size limit → "How did you make it smaller? How did you know when it was small enough?"
  • a rejected photo → "What did the form actually say? How did you get around it?"
  • a transcript / ID / certificate → "Where do you think that file went after you uploaded it?"
  • a scanned handout → "What do you do when you need to quote from one?"
Q3

What device did you start on? What did you finish on?

Q4

Roughly how long did that take, start to finish?

Q5

What was the most annoying part?

Q6

Did anything go wrong that time? What did you do about it?

The workaround is the pain.

The one sentence

Branch questions — ask the ones they open

Aim for 6–8 of these, not all of them. A forced question gets a polite answer, not a pain. Ask the last three of everyone — nothing in the six opens them, and H9 is the one that can prove us wrong.

They named a website or app  ·  H1
  • How did you pick that one?
  • Has a free tool ever stopped you partway — made you wait, pay, or sign up?
  • What did you do when it stopped you?
  • How close was the deadline when that happened?
A transcript / ID / certificate came up  ·  H2 ★
  • Which tool did you use for that one?
  • Where do you think that file went after you uploaded it?
  • Did you do anything differently because of what was in it?
  • Uncomfortable enough to stop and do it another way?
A size limit or “too big”  ·  H5
  • What was the limit?
  • How did you make it smaller? How did you know when it was small enough?
  • How many tries did that take?
A rejected phone photo  ·  H4
  • iPhone or Android?
  • What did the form actually say?
  • How did you get around it?
A scanned handout  ·  H6
  • Can you select or search the text in it?
  • What do you do when you need to quote from one?
  • What language was it in?
Something they do every week  ·  H8
  • Walk me through those steps. Same every time?
  • Ever wished you could just not do them again?
Ask everyone  ·  H3
  • Where were you — campus, dorm, home?
  • Did you notice the upload or download taking a long time?
  • Ever avoided a tool because it was slow on campus wifi?
Ask everyone  ·  H7
  • Besides documents, have you handed in a video or a voice recording?
  • Same site you used for PDFs?
  • How many file tools do you have bookmarked or installed right now?
Ask everyone  ·  H9 ★ can falsify us
  • Do you pay for any file tool now? Has anyone offered you a free student plan?
  • If a paid tool became free tomorrow, would you use it for personal documents too?
  • If they say price is why they'd switch — write it exactly. Do not soften it.

What they raised — unprompted only

Tap Raised only if they brought it up. If you asked about it directly, it does not tick. Two unprompted mentions across two participants promotes H# → P#.
