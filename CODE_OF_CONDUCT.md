# Code of Conduct

UniLab is built by students, for students. We keep it simple:

- **Be welcoming.** Many contributors here are making their first-ever open
  source contribution, in their second or third language. Patience is the
  default; condescension is not tolerated.
- **Critique code, not people.** "This loop blocks the UI" is useful.
  "This is stupid" is not.
- **Respect every language and script.** Thai, Burmese, English and any other
  language are first-class here — dismissing localization or script-handling
  work as "edge cases" goes against the point of the project.
- **No harassment of any kind** — insults, discrimination, unwanted personal
  attention, doxxing. Anywhere in the project's spaces, ever.

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainers via the email on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may remove comments, close threads, or ban contributors who don't follow
these rules.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), v2.1.
